About Orchard Diary ऑर्चर्ड डायरी के बारे में

Orchard Diary is a dedicated knowledge platform created for apple growers of North India. Our goal is to provide practical, science-based orchard management guidance that farmers can easily apply in their fields. We share timely pest alerts, disease identification, seasonal advisories, fertigation planning, and modern production techniques. ऑर्चर्ड डायरी उत्तर भारत के सेब उत्पादकों के लिए बनाया गया एक ज्ञान मंच है। हमारा उद्देश्य वैज्ञानिक और व्यावहारिक बागवानी सलाह प्रदान करना है जिसे किसान आसानी से अपने खेतों में लागू कर सकें।


Through regular updates, articles and field-based recommendations, we aim to help growers reduce losses, manage pests effectively, improve fruit quality and ultimately increase income. Orchard Diary works as your digital extension advisor — always available when you need support. नियमित अपडेट, लेख और फील्ड आधारित सिफारिशों के माध्यम से हम किसानों को नुकसान कम करने, कीटों का बेहतर प्रबंधन करने, फल की गुणवत्ता सुधारने और आय बढ़ाने में मदद करते हैं। ऑर्चर्ड डायरी आपके डिजिटल सलाहकार की तरह हमेशा उपलब्ध है।

"Helping apple growers grow smarter, not harder."

About the Founder

Pawan Kumar

I hold a Master’s degree (M.Sc.) in Fruit Science and have several years of professional experience in horticulture and apple production systems. My work combines scientific research knowledge with practical field-level solutions for orchard growers.

I specialize in fertigation planning, pest and disease diagnosis, orchard nutrition management, canopy training, pruning systems and modern high-density plantation techniques. I have closely worked with farmers to improve productivity, reduce crop losses and enhance fruit quality.

Through Orchard Diary, my mission is to provide reliable, easy-to-understand and science-based advisory services so that every apple grower can make informed decisions and achieve higher yields and profitability.
